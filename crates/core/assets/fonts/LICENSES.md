  redistribute; the font may not be sold by itself; derivatives must not use the "Bitstream Vera" or "DejaVu" names unless they follow the

The SIL Open Font License 1.1 is at https://openfontlicense.org/ . These conversions keep the original family names, as the
fonts' licences allow for unmodified glyph outlines rendered at a fixed size.
